Common questions

Do I need an Oura Ring?

Corio is built for Oura and is at its best with a ring. It also reads Apple Health, but readiness and sleep scores come from your ring, so without one some screens will be limited.

My data isn't syncing

Open You → Integrations and check that your ring shows as connected. If it does, pull down on the Home screen to force a refresh. Oura data for the previous night usually appears in the morning once your ring has synced with the Oura app.

How do I export or delete my data?

Settings → Export My Data gives you everything Corio stores about you. Settings → Delete Account removes your account and all associated data permanently.

How do I cancel a subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple: open the iOS Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and select Corio. Cancelling there stops future renewals.

Something looks wrong in the app

Email us a screenshot. Bug reports with a screenshot get fixed considerably faster than descriptions alone.
